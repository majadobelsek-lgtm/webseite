Research · Study S012026

AI ambient scribing in routine psychiatric care

Evaluating the use of an artificial intelligence ambient scribe in a Swiss psychiatric clinic: efficiency, documentation quality and therapeutic alliance. A nonrandomized controlled trial at Psychiatrie St. Gallen, led by me as principal investigator.

Data collection ongoing · results pending ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07777224

In short

Ambient scribes listen to the conversation between clinician and patient and draft the clinical note. They promise to save time. But psychiatry is built on conversation: the record, the clinician's skills and the patient's trust may all be affected. This study tests what actually changes when clinicians start using an ambient scribe in everyday care.

For researchers

Clinicians using the AlpineAI Listener Tool are compared with clinicians documenting as usual, before and after introduction. Analysis follows difference-in-differences logic with mixed-effects models. Documentation quality is rated by blinded raters. Full methods are in the protocol.
