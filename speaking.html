Speaker bio · short
Dr. med. Maja Dobelsek

Dr. med. Maja Dobelsek is a psychiatrist in training at Psychiatrie St. Gallen and a PhD candidate at the University of St. Gallen (HSG). She leads a controlled trial of an AI ambient scribe in routine psychiatric care across eight sites and works on how AI can improve psychiatry while keeping patients safe and clinicians skilled.
