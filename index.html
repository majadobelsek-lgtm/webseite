About

Clinical work informs the questions I ask.

I studied medicine at the University of Ljubljana and practise psychiatry in Eastern Switzerland. At Psychiatrie St. Gallen I work as acting senior physician in the outpatient department in Wattwil, which I headed in 2026, and I am part of the clinic's digital transformation team. Alongside my specialist training, I am completing a PhD at the University of St. Gallen on AI ambient scribing in psychiatric care.

What drives me: psychiatry can get better with AI, but only if we measure what it does to patients, to clinicians and to the relationship between them.
