About

Clinical work informs the questions I ask.

I'm a psychiatrist in training and a clinician-researcher in Eastern Switzerland. I studied medicine at the University of Ljubljana in Slovenia and came to Swiss psychiatry as an international doctor, an experience that today shapes my course for colleagues who want to take the same path.

Since 2023 I have worked at Psychiatrie St. Gallen, today as acting senior physician in the outpatient department in Wattwil. From January to October 2026 I headed the department: I took on the role at short notice during a phase of severe staff shortages and supported the restructuring and the growth of the team from three to seven clinicians until it was stable. I now focus on my research and my specialist training, and I am part of the clinic's digital transformation team and its AI pilot project.

In parallel I am completing a PhD at the University of St. Gallen (HSG). As principal investigator of study S012026, I lead a controlled evaluation of an AI ambient scribe in routine psychiatric care across eight sites and 47 clinicians.

“Psychiatry can get better with AI, but only if patients stay safe and clinicians stay skilled.”
